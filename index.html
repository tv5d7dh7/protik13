01 Why five

Five ways off the map.

A guide is a small machine for changing your mind about a place. These five change it in five different directions.

One is a ring road through lava, ice and daylight that refuses to behave. One is a Caribbean island with two passports and no particular hurry. One is Portugal in two weeks or less. One is a cruise circuit where the port day is the whole story. One is France at 2,088 pages — the long way, deliberately.

They do not overlap. They do not need to be read in order. Pick the horizon that fits the trip you are actually planning.

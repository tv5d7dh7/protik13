Field notes

Three habits that make coastal clips feel effortless.

1

Find the right light

Sunrise and late afternoon make water glow. At midday, shoot in clear shallows where the color does the work.

2

Open with motion

A wave, a boat or a step into frame. Keep the subject centered in 9:16 and leave room for captions.

3

Respect the shore

Keep your distance from wildlife, stay off dunes and reefs and check local drone and access rules first.
